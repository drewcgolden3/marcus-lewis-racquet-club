Fixing the front desk problem

One place to book, and one place to ask

With the physical front desk gone, the site is the front desk. Right now a new player lands on a contact page with twenty staff email addresses and has to guess which one to use. This replaces all of that with one booking screen and one form that routes itself to the right coordinator.

About the club

Everyone means everyone.

Younger or older, beginner or advanced, competitive or just out for the fun of it. The Marcus Lewis Racquet Club was built on the idea that racquet sports should not be reserved for people who already know how to play.

Between the West facility on Nonset Path, satellite programs across Acton, Boxborough, Carlisle, Westford, Chelmsford, Harvard and Sudbury, and the new fourteen-court East club opening in 2026, we have grown into the area's leading tennis and pickleball provider.
